What every screen keeps

Every value, in both schemes, on the Tokens board The rail The made-up picture

Neutral greys around pictures. Colour belongs to the pictures; the interface keeps one volt accent for the thing to press and for what's picked.

The rail on the side. Home, Library, Queue (with how far the batch has got), History, and Settings at its foot. A status bar under every screen says what the graphics card is doing.

Status by shape. done is solid, working turns, waiting is hollow, a problem is hatched.

Numbers in Plex Mono. Sizes, pixels, times and counts line up and read at a glance: 7680 × 4320 · 18 MB · 6 min.

Keyboard first. Everything works without a mouse, with a 2 px volt focus ring you can always see. Click targets are 36 px or more.

Motion that explains. 120 ms for a press, 200 ms for a toggle, 320 ms for a panel, one curve, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); nothing moves when Windows' animation effects are off.
