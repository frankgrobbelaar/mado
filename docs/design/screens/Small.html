THE SMALL THINGS

When something gets in the way

Each one names the problem, says what Mado already did, and offers the next step. Notes sit at the window's foot; what stops a job is a card in its place; what stops Mado fills the window.

A file in use · a note at the foot

Harbour at dusk.jpg is open in another appMado can't replace it while it's open. Close it there and Mado tries again by itself.

Offline · a note at the foot

You're offlineEverything works, all on this PC. Only looking for a new version waits till you're back.

Not enough space · a card in the queue

A picture that won't open · a card in its place

The engine stopped · fills the window

Mado's engine stopped It tried twice. Your pictures are untouched; what was in the queue waits. The paging file is too small for this operation to complete.

Records damaged past use · fills the window

Mado's records are damaged A disk fault or a power cut, most likely: SQLite says file is not a database. Your pictures are fine; only Mado's notes about them are hurt. The newest backup that opens is from today at 03:00. Either way the damaged file stays beside them as it is: mado (damaged 2026-11-22 0930).db
