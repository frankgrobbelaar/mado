Three looks for Mado. Pick one.

Each look shows the same four places: Home, the Library, a picture with its tools, and many pictures at once. All three keep neutral greys around your pictures, so the window never changes how a photo's colours look. The one you pick becomes Mado's design, and every other screen follows it.

What all three share

  • Neutral greys around every picture. One small accent, kept for the one thing to press and for what's picked.
  • Right for every screen. A picture made for a phone, a tablet, a laptop, a monitor, an ultrawide or an 8K TV, or for print: its size, shape and quality.
  • The same places and words. Home, Library, Queue, History and Settings, in plain words: "Make it for a screen", "Looks", "Cut out", "Many at once".
  • Motion that explains. 120 ms for a press, 200 ms for a toggle, 320 ms for a panel, one curve; nothing moves when Windows' animation effects are off.
  • Status by shape. Done is solid, working is a turning ring, waiting is hollow, a problem is hatched.
  • Light and dark, and the keyboard. Each look comes in both, following Windows unless you choose; everything works without a mouse, with a focus ring you can always see.
